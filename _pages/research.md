My research connects control algorithms with real vehicles, roadside infrastructure, communication systems, and field data. The goal is to make connected and automated transportation more energy-efficient, resilient, scalable, and safe.

## Research themes

### Robust and sustainable vehicle control

I develop eco-driving and optimal-control methods for automated vehicles, then evaluate how those methods respond to realistic disturbances. This work spans trajectory planning, adversarial resilience evaluation, hardware-in-the-loop testing, and closed-track experiments.

### Vehicles as mobile sensing platforms

I investigate how production and research vehicles can sense road and weather conditions while driving. Recent applications include tire-road friction estimation, roadway-condition screening, traffic-state estimation, and visibility classification for severe-weather warnings.

### Cooperative and connected mobility

I integrate V2X communication, cooperative perception, vehicle control, and smart infrastructure. My work includes C-V2X performance testing, FHWA CARMA deployment, visual-enhanced cooperative traffic operations, and infrastructure readiness for cooperative driving automation.

## Selected sponsored research

### Evaluating Energy and Mobility Impacts of CAVs - SMART 2.0

**Argonne National Laboratory · $223,000 · Oct. 2022-Jun. 2024**

I helped write the proposal; developed and calibrated a vehicle data-acquisition system combining CAN bus, GNSS/IMU, and V2X hardware; compared PC5 and LTE-Uu communications; integrated eco-driving controllers with real-time vehicle interfaces; and conducted closed-track experiments. [Controller repository](https://github.com/CATS-Lab/CATS-Lab-AV-Development-AutowareV10.0_Source_Code/tree/ANL-controller).

### Roadway Friction Screening and Measurement with Automated Vehicles

**Center for Connected and Automated Transportation · $100,000 · May 2024-Aug. 2025**

I investigated automated vehicles as mobile probes for real-time roadway-friction monitoring. The work combined optimal control for controlled high-slip maneuvers with tire-dynamics modeling to connect vehicle response to surface-friction characteristics.

### Severe Weather Connected Vehicle Warning System

**Wisconsin Department of Transportation · $250,000 · Sep. 2025**

I developed an in-vehicle sensing data pipeline and a computer-vision method for visibility classification.

### Visual-Enhanced Cooperative Traffic Operations (VECTOR)

**U.S. Department of Energy · $850,000 · Oct. 2021-Jan. 2025**

Working with Connected Wise LLC, I supported proposal development, visual sensing and machine-learning integration, hardware-software co-design, and real-world system deployment for scalable cooperative driving automation. [Related repository](https://github.com/CATS-Lab/DOE-Communication_by_binary_code).

I deployed the FHWA CARMA platform on a research vehicle, integrated onboard computing and vehicle-control interfaces, configured ROS-based components, and conducted field testing for cooperative automated-driving applications.
