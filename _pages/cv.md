## Selected research experience

- **SMART 2.0 - Energy and Mobility Impacts of CAVs:** integrated CAN, GNSS/IMU, V2X, and vehicle-control systems; evaluated PC5 and LTE-Uu communications; and validated eco-driving controllers through closed-track testing.
- **Roadway friction screening with automated vehicles:** developed optimal-control methods for high-slip maneuvers and used tire-dynamics models to estimate surface-friction characteristics.
- **Severe-weather connected-vehicle warning:** built an in-vehicle sensing pipeline and a computer-vision visibility classifier.
- **VECTOR cooperative traffic operations:** integrated visual sensing and machine learning with cooperative driving-automation infrastructure.
- **FHWA CARMA:** deployed CARMA on research vehicles and supported field testing for TSMO and Integrated Highway Prototype use cases.
- **Connected-vehicle cybersecurity:** modeled attacks on onboard and roadside units and evaluated mitigation strategies.

See the [research page](/research/) for project sponsors, dates, and details.
